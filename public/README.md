# Cosmic bridge assets

`brand/fold.svg` is the selected vector master. The Aperture trial is retained at `brand/aperture.svg`. The previous `brand/carved-b.svg` is retained. `brand/split-b-backup.svg` retains option 3. `brand/app-icon.svg` and the platform icons under `src-tauri/icons` are generated with `node scripts/generate-brand.mjs` from the Fold master. The concept comparison is preserved in `docs/plans/assets/brigadier-abstract-mark-options.png`.

`audio/welcome.m4a` is the original synthesized 22-second ambient score used in the approved motion concept; `audio/return.m4a` is a 4.5-second return cue derived from that score. Both are finite tracks; the interface fades playback when leaving the intro. No audio or graphic asset was extracted from Arc's video.

The procedural light field lives in `src/components/CosmicField.tsx`. Its color formula and shape timing follow the approved Cosmic bridge study in `docs/plans/assets/cosmic-bridge-motion-reference.mp4`.
